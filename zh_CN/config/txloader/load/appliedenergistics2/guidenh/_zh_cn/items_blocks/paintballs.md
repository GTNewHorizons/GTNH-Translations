Paintballs are used in a <ItemLink id="appliedenergistics2:item.ToolColorApplicator" /> to paint colorable blocks such as [cables](cables.md), wool, terracotta, glass, and concrete. They can also be fired from a <ItemLink id="appliedenergistics2:item.ToolMassCannon" />.
