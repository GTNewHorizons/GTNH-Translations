# Crystal Growth Accelerator

<BlockImage id="appliedenergistics2:tile.BlockQuartzGrowthAccelerator" scale="4" />

The crystal growth accelerator speeds the growth of Fluix, Certus Quartz, and Nether Quartz seeds. When water, including flowing water, touches a working accelerator, seeds growing in that water receive the acceleration.

It runs on AE energy. An energy acceptor or ME controller can convert other energy systems, and the accelerator can connect to an ME network. Only the top and bottom black faces accept power; the other four faces do not. It consumes 8 AE/t and flashes purple when powered.

For manual power, place an <ItemLink id="appliedenergistics2:item.ItemMultiMaterial:46" /> (wooden crank) on the top or bottom and right-click. Cables connect only at the ends with pink Fluix decorations.
