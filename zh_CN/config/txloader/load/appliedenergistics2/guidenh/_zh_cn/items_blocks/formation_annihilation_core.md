# Formation and Annihilation Cores

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="appliedenergistics2:item.ItemMultiMaterial:43" scale="4" />
  <ItemImage id="appliedenergistics2:item.ItemMultiMaterial:44" scale="4" />

These are the primary components of AE2 input and output [devices](../ae2_mechanics/devices.md). Together with <ItemLink id="appliedenergistics2:item.ItemMultiMaterial:7" /> and a logic processor, they give devices the ability to input and output items, blocks, fluids, and more. They have no standalone function and are crafting intermediates.
