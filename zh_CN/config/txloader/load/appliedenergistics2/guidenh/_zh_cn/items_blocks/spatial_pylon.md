# The Spatial Pylon

<BlockImage id="appliedenergistics2:tile.BlockSpatialPylon"/>

The Spatial Pylon is used in [Spatial IO](../ae2_mechanics/spatial_io.md) to generate the spatial field and define the affected volume. Each continuous line uses one [channel](../ae2_mechanics/channels.md), and lines must be at least two blocks long to function.
